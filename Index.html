Clarity matters

Serious about safety. Honest about limits.

Ona Polisi is a reporting and accountability tool. It is not an emergency service, a replacement for legal advice, or a guarantee of prosecution.

  • Use emergency services if someone is in immediate danger.
  • Share only information that is safe for you to record.
  • Save your case reference somewhere you can access later.
